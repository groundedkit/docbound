# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

Welcome to Northwind Labs. This handbook explains the policies that apply to all full-time employees. Part-time employees and contractors should check their individual agreements, which take precedence where they differ. If anything here is unclear, ask the People Operations team in the #people-ops channel.

## 1. Working Hours and Location

Our standard working week is 40 hours. We do not track hours, but we expect you to be reachable during core collaboration hours, which run from 10:00 to 15:00 in your team's home time zone. Outside core hours you can arrange your day however works best for you, as long as your team knows how to reach you.

### Hybrid and remote work

Employees attached to an office may work remotely up to three days per week. Your manager can approve a temporary fully remote arrangement of up to eight weeks, for example during a family move. Permanent fully remote arrangements require approval from a director and a signed remote work agreement.

Working from another country is limited to 30 days in any 12-month period because of tax and immigration rules. Tell People Operations at least two weeks before you travel so they can check the destination.

### Home office equipment

Every new employee receives a one-time home office stipend of 900 euros for a desk, chair, monitor and other equipment. Keep your receipts; unused stipend money does not roll over. Laptops are provided by IT and remain company property.

## 2. Time Off

### Vacation

Full-time employees receive 27 days of paid vacation per calendar year, plus public holidays in their country of employment. Vacation accrues monthly. You may carry up to five unused days into the next year; carried-over days expire if they are not used by March 31.

Requests for more than ten consecutive working days must be approved by your manager at least six weeks in advance. Shorter requests only need to be entered in the HR system before you leave.

### Sick leave

There is no fixed limit on paid sick days. Let your manager know as early as possible on the first day you are unwell. If you are sick for more than three consecutive working days, upload a doctor's note to the HR system.

### Parental leave

Birthing parents receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Non-birthing parents receive 12 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken in up to three blocks within the first 18 months after the child's birth or adoption. Parental leave is available from your first day of employment.

## 3. Compensation and Benefits

Salaries are paid monthly on the 25th. If the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday, payment is made on the previous working day. Salary reviews happen once a year in April, based on the leveling framework and market data.

### Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,200 euros for courses, books, certifications and conference tickets. Conference attendance also includes up to three days of paid time that does not count against your vacation. Unused learning budget does not carry over. After attending a conference, share a short summary in the engineering newsletter.

### Wellbeing allowance

Employees receive a monthly wellbeing allowance of 60 euros that can be spent on gym memberships, sports clubs, therapy apps or mental health services. Claim it through the expense tool with the category "Wellbeing".

## 4. Expenses and Travel

Submit expenses within 45 days through the expense tool, with an itemized receipt for every item over 10 euros. Expenses submitted later than 45 days may be rejected.

When travelling for work, meals are reimbursed up to 70 euros per day. Book economy class for flights shorter than six hours; premium economy may be booked for longer flights. Hotels should cost no more than 180 euros per night in most cities, or 250 euros per night in London, Zurich and New York. Alcohol, minibar purchases and personal entertainment are not reimbursable.

## 5. Security

All laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after five minutes of inactivity. Use the company password manager for every work account and enable two-factor authentication wherever it is offered.

Report suspicious emails with the Report Phish button in your mail client. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the security team within one hour by calling the security hotline, so the device can be wiped remotely.

Access to production databases is granted only through a just-in-time access request. Approved access expires automatically after four hours, and every query is logged for audit purposes.

## 6. Leaving Northwind Labs

The notice period is one month during the six-month probation period and three months afterwards. Return your laptop and badge on your last day; IT will send a prepaid shipping box to remote employees. Unused vacation days are paid out with your final salary.
